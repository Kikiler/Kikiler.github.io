The Crew — Season 01

Meet the founders & engineering leads behind ESET.

From a sketch in a school garage to the international starting grid. Meet the founders, co-founders, and multidisciplinary leads designing, welding, and wiring our solar endurance race car.

Leadership

Thibaut Dochen

Founder & Team Principal

Spearheaded the creation of ESET after identifying the lack of student automotive projects. Directs team strategy, administration relations, race calendar planning, and cross-team execution.

Team Direction Strategy Operations
Chassis & Mechanical

Mathieu Noblesse

Co-Founder & Chassis Lead

Heads tubular spaceframe engineering, suspension kinematics, braking circuits, and pilot cockpit ergonomics. Oversees workshop welding, CNC machining, and structural torsion testing.

Spaceframe Suspension TIG Welding
Powertrain & Electrical

Guillaume Warichet

Co-Founder & Powertrain Lead

Architect of the high-voltage drivetrain, custom battery pack modules, Battery Management System (BMS), inverter tuning, and the low-voltage vehicle harness loom.

High-Voltage Battery BMS Telemetry
Aerodynamics

Julie Balfroid

Co-Founder & Aerodynamics Lead

Conducts computational fluid dynamics (CFD) drag modeling, surface curvature refinement, and composite shell mold fabrication to maximize aerodynamic endurance on track.

CFD Simulation Carbon Fiber Drag Reduction
Solar & Energy

Oscar Vuylsteke

Co-Founder & Solar Array Lead

Engineers the rooftop photovoltaic array layout, Maximum Power Point Tracking (MPPT) converters, and energy budget prediction models to harvest peak wattage across changing race conditions.

PV Arrays MPPT Converters Energy Budgeting
Operations & Media

Diandra Lahaeye

Co-Founder & Operations Lead

Manages corporate sponsor acquisition, industry partnerships, external media presence, and paddock logistics to ensure the crew has every tool, part, and connection required to compete.

Sponsorship Logistics Communications
Faculty Advisory

Philippe Melotte

Faculty Advisor & Mentor

Professor at ECAM Brussels providing technical supervision, safety regulations compliance, academic sponsorship, and vital liaison with university laboratories and tooling facilities.

Academic Oversight Safety Compliance Engineering Mentorship
